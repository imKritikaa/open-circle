About us ✦

Different people. Same curiosity.

Open Circle is MAIT’s open source society. We’re students who like to learn in public, build real things, and share what we know.

Why open source?

Anyone can join the circle.

Open source is software that anyone can read, use, change and improve. It’s also one of the friendliest ways to learn: real code, real reviews, real people who want you to get better.

We run workshops for first-timers, build projects together, and help members make their first contributions to projects around the world. You don’t need to be an expert. You need to be curious.

Everything we make is shared in the open, so the next person can start where we finished.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on Git, Linux, web development, AI tools and more. Bring a laptop and a question.
